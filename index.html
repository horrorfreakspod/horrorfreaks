The show

Two freaks. One movie. Every week.

Horror Freaks is a podcast for people who love horror movies as much as we do. Every week we pick a different film and walk through it from the opening credits to the final scare.

Along the way we dig up the behind-the-scenes stories, production trivia, and any extra fun stuff we can find about the movie. Classics, cult favorites, slashers, creature features, and whatever else keeps us up at night.

Your hosts

Meet the freaks

Big J Millz

Born and raised in Oklahoma. Started watching scary movies at the tender age of 7, when his amazing mother taught him that what you see on the screen isn't always real. From there, his love for horror took off! Growing up on the most gruesome and scary films, J Millz discovered that not everything that goes bump in the dark can get you.

Practical effects forever

A true lover of practical effects: the less CGI, the better!

Loves all kinds of horror, from the deep slashers to the corny killers. If it's horror, J Millz is IN!!

J-Rod

Born and raised in Cincinnati, Ohio, J-Rod has been a horror freak for as long as he can remember. His love for the genre started as a kid, watching horror movies with his mom and dad. Those early movie nights sparked a lifelong fascination with everything horror.

From classic slashers and supernatural thrillers to all the newest releases, J-Rod loves it all!!!
